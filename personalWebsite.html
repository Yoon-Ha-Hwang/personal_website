Projects

You can see a lot of my projects on my Github profile. (yes I did join on roughly the end of September)

Here is a short list of my featured projects, links to repositories:

  • Console-Based Card Game

This is a project I worked on (also for Stardance) where I developed an intelligent bot called "Intellisense". How it works: she (Intellisense is female) analyses each valid move, rates all of them, and then chooses the move with the greatest rating.

Of course Intellisense might look quite simple because there aren't so many moves to analyse (just look at Chess or Go, they have like millions of valid moves), but I still feel proud of it because it's quite easy to scale up and I got to know the art of game bots.
